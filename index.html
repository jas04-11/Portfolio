🌫AboutMe

I am in my final year of the Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) program. Throughout my time in this program, I've built a strong skill set in various programming languages, particularly C++ and Java, as well as front-end web development. I had the great opportunity to intern as a web developer at VaultofCodes, where I worked on creating dynamic and responsive websites. This experience has not only enhanced my technical skills but also taught me the importance of teamwork and effective communication in a professional setting. In addition to my academic and internship experiences, I have undertaken several projects. These projects have allowed me to apply my knowledge in HTML, CSS3, JavaScript, and frameworks like Bootstrap, while also exploring my interest in user-centric design. I am deeply passionate about the potential of technology to simplify and improve everyday life. As I prepare to graduate, I am eager to continue expanding my knowledge, tackling new challenges, and contributing to innovative technological solutions.
